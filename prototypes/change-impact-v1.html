How this console works
From queue to launched workflow in four steps
1
Change queue
Every open engineering change in a single list, with the item on hold, originator, type, status, and how many order and sales lines it touches. Pick one to scope the console.
2
Impact
Four levers side by side: material committed, stock exposure, work in the plant, customer commitments. Each shows the choice in words, the dollar figure, counts, and a status. Blockers are marked.
3
Approve and launch
Short. The change in one line, the two people in the first steps, one Approve button, and the route that will run after you approve.
4
Workflow status
Pick a change and read where its route stands. Done, now, or not started per step, with who holds it and how long. One plain line says what is next.
